🐄 HerdEye

AI Powered Livestock Health Monitoring for Farmers

**HerdEye** is an AI-powered livestock health monitoring system that uses cameras and computer vision to monitor individual cows and buffaloes, detect early changes in health and behaviour, and alert farmers before problems become serious.

> From observing animals manually to giving every animal its own health story.

---

🌾 The Problem

Farmers often identify livestock health problems only after visible symptoms appear.

By then, they may already face:

- Reduced milk production
- Higher veterinary costs
- Delayed treatment
- Livestock loss
- Difficulty monitoring every animal individually

Most existing monitoring solutions depend on wearable sensors or collars, which can be expensive, require maintenance, and are not always practical for small and medium-sized farmers.

---

 💡 Our Solution

HerdEye uses **camera based AI** to continuously observe livestock without requiring wearable devices.

The system monitors individual animals and looks for changes in:

- 🐄 Movement and activity
- 🍽️ Feeding behaviour
- 🦷 Rumination / chewing patterns
- 💤 Resting and standing behaviour
- 🔊 Vocal behaviour
- 🚶 Walking and gait patterns
- 📊 Changes from the animal's own historical behaviour

Instead of simply saying **"something is wrong with the herd,"** HerdEye aims to identify **which animal is showing an abnormal change and why it matters.**

🐮 Animal-Level Monitoring

Every animal can have its own digital health record.

For example:

```text
Animal ID: #08
Tag: Red

Status: ⚠️ Attention Required

Observed:
• Reduced rumination
• Lower activity
• Reduced feeding

Compared with:
• Animal's previous behaviour

HerdEye:
• Generates an alert
• Provides supporting video evidence
• Allows the farmer to share the case with a veterinarian
```

The goal is to move from **herd-level observation to animal-level intelligence.**

---

🚨 Early Alerts

When HerdEye detects an unusual change, the farmer can receive an alert containing:

- Animal ID
- Type of abnormal behaviour
- Duration of the change
- Health risk information
- Short evidence video
- Option to share the case with a veterinarian

This gives the farmer something actionable rather than just raw camera footage.

 🩺 Farmer + Veterinarian Workflow
